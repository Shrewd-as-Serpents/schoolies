# Master Reference Key: Internal Citation & Theoretical Index
## Project: *Decoding Absences: A Decolonial Framework for Textual Reconstruction*

This master reference key is designed for internal project mapping across the literature review, methodology, website database (`sage-audit-data.json`), and coding protocols. Each entry includes an **Internal Reference ID (`[REF-XXX-YEAR]`)**, full APA citation, key theoretical concepts, project function, and primary source mapping within the notebook.

## Section 1: Decolonial Epistemology & Sociology of Absences

### `[REF-SAN-2014]` De Sousa Santos, B. (2014)
* **Full Citation**: de Sousa Santos, B. (2014). *Epistemologies of the South: Justice against epistemicide*. Routledge.
* **Key Concepts**: Sociology of Absences, Sociology of Emergencies, Abyssal Line, 5 Monocultures (Knowledge/Rigor, Linear Time, Naturalized Classification, Dominant Scale, Capitalist Productivity), 5 Ecologies (Knowledges, Temporalities, Recognition, Trans-scale, Productivities), Intercultural Translation.
* **Project Function**: Core theoretical foundation for Tier 1 deconstructive absence coding and Tier 4 reconstructive ecology mapping.
* **Notebook Source**: `EBSCO-FullText-08_30_2026 (2).pdf`, `martamorgado,+03_RPE_36_1_23880.pdf`, `Deconstructive-Reconstructive Text Analysis Using Santos's Monocultures.md`.

### `[REF-SAN-2007]` De Sousa Santos, B. (2007)
* **Full Citation**: de Sousa Santos, B. (2007). Beyond abyssal thinking: From global lines to ecologies of knowledges. *Review (Fernand Braudel Center)*, 30(1), 45–89.
* **Key Concepts**: Abyssal Thinking, Epistemicide, Ecology of Knowledges, Subaltern Cosmopolitanism.
* **Project Function**: Defines the "abyssal line" that divides visible/sanctioned knowledge from invisible/disqualified knowledge.

### `[REF-GRO-2013]` Grosfoguel, R. (2013)
* **Full Citation**: Grosfoguel, R. (2013). The structure of knowledge in Westernized universities: Epistemic racism/sexism and the four genocides/epistemicides of the long 16th century. *Human Architecture: Journal of the Sociology of Self-Knowledge*, 11(1), 73–90.
* **Key Concepts**: Four Genocides/Epistemicides of the Long 16th Century (Al-Andalus conquest, Indigenous genocide in the Americas, African enslavement, European witch-hunts), Epistemic Racism/Sexism, Westernized University Canon.
* **Project Function**: Historical bias-lens cross-referencing (Tier 3 coding) that grounds Santos's abstract monocultures in documented historical violence.
* **Notebook Source**: `SOCH3005-litreview-outline.md`, `Literature Review and Citation Review`.

### `[REF-GRO-2007]` Grosfoguel, R. (2007)
* **Full Citation**: Grosfoguel, R. (2007). The epistemic decolonial turn: Beyond political-economy paradigms. *Cultural Studies*, 21(2-3), 211–223.
* **Key Concepts**: Coloniality of Power, Geopolitics of Knowledge, Body-Politics of Knowledge.
* **Project Function**: Establishes that location of enunciation (*where* one speaks from) shapes the validity claims of research.

### `[REF-CAS-2010]` Castro-Gómez, S. (2010)
* **Full Citation**: Castro-Gómez, S. (2010). *La hybris del punto zero: Ciencia, raza e ilustración en la Nueva Granada (1750-1816)*. Editorial Pontificia Universidad Javeriana.
* **Key Concepts**: *La hybris del punto zero* (The Hubris of the Zero Point), Unobserved Observer, Enlightenment Science & Coloniality.
* **Project Function**: Theoretical critique of objectivity; used to unmask the unlocated, disembodied voice of research textbooks.

### `[REF-QUI-2000]` Quijano, A. (2000)
* **Full Citation**: Quijano, A. (2000). Coloniality of power, Eurocentrism, and Latin America. *Nepantla: Views from South*, 1(3), 533–580.
* **Key Concepts**: Coloniality of Power, Eurocentrism, Social Classification, Racial Capitalism.
* **Project Function**: Informs the *Monoculture of Naturalized Classification* (`MONO_CLASS`).

### `[REF-MAL-2017]` Maldonado-Torres, N. (2017)
* **Full Citation**: Maldonado-Torres, N. (2017). Frantz Fanon and the decolonial turn in psychology: From modern/colonial methods to the decolonial attitude. *South African Journal of Psychology*, 47(4), 432–441.
* **Key Concepts**: Coloniality of Being, Decolonial Attitude, Methodological Captivity.
* **Project Function**: Analyzes how rigid insistence on standard methods functions as a technology of ontological control.

### `[REF-SMI-2012]` Smith, L. T. (2012)
* **Full Citation**: Smith, L. T. (2012). *Decolonizing methodologies: Research and Indigenous peoples* (2nd ed.). Zed Books.
* **Key Concepts**: Imperial Research, Extraction, Indigenous Knowledge Systems, Counter-Research Protocols.
* **Project Function**: Foundational text for critiquing Western academic research as an instrument of empire.

### `[REF-TUC-2012]` Tuck, E., & Yang, K. W. (2012)
* **Full Citation**: Tuck, E., & Yang, K. W. (2012). Decolonization is not a metaphor. *Decolonization: Indigeneity, Education & Society*, 1(1), 1–40.
* **Key Concepts**: Settler Colonialism, Settler Moves to Innocence, Material Land Restitution vs. Metaphorical Decolonization.
* **Project Function**: Prevents decolonial terminology from being reduced to a superficial academic buzzword.

## Section 4: Literary Testimonio, Genre Trouble, & Discursive Injustice

### `[REF-SPI-1988]` Spivak, G. C. (1988)
* **Full Citation**: Spivak, G. C. (1988). Can the subaltern speak? In C. Nelson & L. Grossberg (Eds.), *Marxism and the interpretation of culture* (pp. 271–313). University of Illinois Press.
* **Key Concepts**: Subaltern Muteness, Epistemic Violence of Representation, Colonial Subject Constitution.
* **Project Function**: Highlights the trap of speaking *for* marginalized people within Eurocentric structures.

### `[REF-LYO-2002]` Lyotard, J.-F. (2002)
* **Full Citation**: Lyotard, J.-F. (2002). *The differend: Phrases in dispute*. University of Minnesota Press.
* **Key Concepts**: The *Differend*, Discursive Injustice, Incommensurability of Phrase Regimes.
* **Project Function**: Explains how victim testimonies are invalidated when forced to conform to the perpetrator's rules of discourse.

### `[REF-DAN-1998]` Danticat, E. (1998)
* **Full Citation**: Danticat, E. (1998). *The farming of bones*. Penguin Books.
* **Key Concepts**: Testimonial Fiction, 1937 Parsley Massacre, Retelling Survivor Stories, "In a language that is theirs, not yours".
* **Project Function**: Literary demonstration of the double-bind faced by traumatized storytellers.
* **Notebook Source**: `DECOLONIZING_STRAIGHT_TEMPORAL.pdf`.
